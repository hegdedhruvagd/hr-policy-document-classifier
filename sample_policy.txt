Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave every year. Sick leave requires approval from the manager.

Monthly salaries are credited on the last working day of each month. Employees are eligible for medical insurance benefits.

All candidates must submit their resumes during recruitment. Shortlisted applicants will be invited for interviews.

Harassment and discrimination are strictly prohibited in the workplace. Employees must maintain professional conduct.

Employees must submit travel expense claims with receipts within 30 days. Manager approval is required before reimbursement.
